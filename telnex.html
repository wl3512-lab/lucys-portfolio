Design  ·  2024

Telnex

Honourable Mention — 2025 Scholastic Art and Writing Awards

Medium Soap, Adobe Photoshop
Year 2024
Category Design

Inspired by the 1999 film Fight Club, this piece imagines an advertisement for Telnex, a fictional communication device used by the film's characters. At its center is a bar of soap carved into the shape of a rotary telephone, merging symbols of cleansing and communication to reflect the film's subversive tone.

The yellow-green palette echoes the sterile lighting of industrial environments, evoking isolation and emotional detachment. The slogan "Uncover You, Connect Two" functions as both a brand line and a metaphor for the protagonist's fractured identity.

The work explores Fight Club's recurring themes of duality, illusion, and the longing for authentic connection within a fragmented, material world.
