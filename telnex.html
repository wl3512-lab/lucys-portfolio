Statement

I designed an advertisement for a fictional communication device inspired by Fight Club. I carved a bar of soap into a rotary telephone, photographed it, and built the poster in Photoshop.

I used a yellow-green palette drawn from the film’s industrial interiors. The slogan “Uncover You, Connect Two” refers to the protagonist’s split identity.
