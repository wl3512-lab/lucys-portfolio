Fine Arts  ·  2024

Anticipation

Medium Oil on canvas, spray paint
Year 2024
Category Fine Arts

Image coming soon

This painting delves into the glorification and commodification of Marilyn Monroe as an emblem of beauty, femininity, and vulnerability. By depicting her in a moment of self-reflection, I aim to highlight the internal conflict between her true self and the image imposed upon her by society.

Highlighting the constricting nature of these expectations — how they shape and often distort one's identity. This piece is deeply personal to me because it resonates with the universal experience of grappling with external pressures and the quest for self-acceptance.

It is a commentary on the broader cultural obsession with perfection and the often-overlooked cost of maintaining such façades.
