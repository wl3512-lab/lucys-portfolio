UX Design  ·  2023

SoundCloud
App Critique

A critique of three areas in the SoundCloud iOS app — brand identity, home screen widgets, and playlist management — with proposed redesigns for each.

Type App critique
Platform SoundCloud iOS
Focus areas Branding, widget customization, playlist UI
Year 2023
01

SoundCloud's orange carries nostalgia but limits individual expression. A user-defined color system would let listeners make the app feel like their own.

02

Competitors offer more flexible home screen widgets. Expanded customization would surface liked content and give SoundCloud a stronger foothold on the lock screen.

03

A redesigned widget menu and dedicated likes toolbar cut the steps between a user and the tracks they return to most.

04

Auto-generated playlist covers flatten personal curation. User-controlled cover art better reflects the taste and intent behind each collection.
