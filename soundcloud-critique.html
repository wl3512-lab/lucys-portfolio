SoundCloud
App Critique

A critique of three areas in the SoundCloud iOS app: brand identity, home screen widgets, and playlist management, with proposed redesigns for each.

Type App critique
Platform SoundCloud iOS
Focus areas Branding, widget customization, playlist UI
Year 2023
01

When SoundCloud replaced its iconic orange with black, it broke 15 years of visual memory. Keeping the orange cloud on the new black background would have preserved the recognition without abandoning the rebrand.

02

No major competitor currently offers in-app widget customization. Adding control over layout, toolbar order, and icon sizing would reinforce ownership, surface what listeners return to most, and give SoundCloud a differentiator no streaming app has claimed yet.

03

A redesigned widget menu and dedicated likes toolbar cut the steps between a user and the tracks they return to most.

04

The playlist cover was visually lost: undersized against same-height track artwork, with dead space to its right. Enlarging it and shifting the metadata across fills that gap and gives curated collections the visual weight that encourages ownership.
