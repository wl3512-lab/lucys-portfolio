04 / Design Principles

Brand Pillars

Three ideas that define how the brand thinks, feels, and communicates.

01
Liminal

Existing at the threshold — between the physical and digital, the intuitive and the technical. The work lives in that in-between space.

02
Precise

Every decision is intentional. Experimental doesn't mean chaotic — it means considered risk-taking with clear purpose behind each choice.

03
Human

The futurism is always in service of people. Accessibility and human-centered design anchor every aesthetic choice, however bold.

08 / Voice & Tone

Brand Voice

The brand speaks the way Lucy thinks — sharp, warm, experimental, never pretentious.

Do
→ Lead with specificity — "interaction patterns for spatial computing" not "cool tech stuff"
→ Own the tension between experimental and accessible
→ Use lowercase typography throughout — it's a style choice, not an accident
→ Reference the methodology: user research, accessibility audits, co-design
→ Sound curious, not authoritative
Don't
→ Use buzzword soup — "synergy," "disruptive," "innovative solutions"
→ Over-explain the aesthetic — let the work speak
→ Capitalize randomly or break the lowercase system
→ Use exclamation marks (the work is confident, not excitable)
→ Write in the passive voice — own your decisions
