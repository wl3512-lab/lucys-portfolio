Agar Alphabet

Progress Through Process

Medium
Agar plates, live bacteria, Adobe Photoshop
Year
2025
Category
Design

I carved letters and the phrase “Progress Through Process” into agar plates, then grew bacteria from samples collected from my breath and the studio.

I documented the growth, changed the conditions when letters failed to develop, and kept the uneven results as part of the alphabet.
