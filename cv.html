Driftwood · AI ethics game

Spring – Sep 2026

Solo Designer & Developer, NYU IMA · Read the case study

  • A 15-minute browser game: players adopt an AI pet with a hidden flaw (sycophancy, gaslighting, hallucination, reckless advice), name it, and write the rules that constrain it.
  • Pets read the player's face locally with face-api.js, disclosed up front; a mood-access dial turns data minimization into a choice the player can see.
  • Mid-build interviews showed the chatbot format felt clinical; rebuilt it as a full-screen pixel pet world. About 76% of 100+ visitors named their pet's flaw unprompted.
  • Drew the title screen, SVG training-room scene, and certificate screen. The first build ran on the OpenAI API; the Jun to Sep rebuild runs offline in kiosk mode with no account, key, or server.
