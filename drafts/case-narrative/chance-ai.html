Redesigning the Chance AI app

↓ See the final design

Overview

Chance AI is a live app on iOS and Android that answers questions about a photo: people snap anything, get an answer, or run a visual skill another user made. Through AStudio at NYU IMA, I led our team of five in redesigning it with the client.

Problems

Three problems shaped the redesign.

  1. 1. Chatbot

    The result screen read like a ChatGPT reply: a long paragraph first, the wrong colour on the product, an ask bar like a chat composer.

  2. 2. Catalog

    Visual skills looked like a catalog of tools, not a living gallery.

  3. 3. Lopsided

    The carousel of skills sat on one big Snap to know button, though the client also wants more people on visual skills.

Opportunity

How might Chance AI answer like a product, not a chatbot, and give visual skills equal weight?

Goals

Each problem became a goal.

  • Chatbot→Product

    The result leads with the product, its price and Exact match.

  • Catalog→Gallery

    Skills on full-photo cards, with their creators.

  • Lopsided→Equal

    Snap to know and Create visual skill side by side, at the same size.

What I learned

  • Tie each change to a finding.

    Every change in the wireframes carries the number of the audit finding it fixes, so anyone reviewing can see why it changed.

  • Name the question before the screens.

    Writing each page’s How might we gave the team one test for every week’s version.

  • Next is testing with students.

    Most users are college students, so the next round is sessions with them on the new Home and Visual skills.
