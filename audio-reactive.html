How it's built

How I map audio to motion. I start with audioAnalysis.tox, a community TouchDesigner container that extracts frequency-band values. I smooth the channels with a Lag CHOP and remap their ranges to control circle radius and corner-pin displacement. I then blend the distorted image with the previous frame to create trails. Adjusting the smoothing and feedback opacity changes how long each hit remains visible.
