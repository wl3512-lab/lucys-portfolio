Arrested Motion

Medium
Oil on canvas
Year
2023
Size
18 × 22 in
Category
Fine Arts

I painted hands washing dishes to study movement within a repeated task. I used transparent layers and brush texture to show the hands moving through the water.

I worked on holding a single moment while retaining traces of the motions before and after it.
