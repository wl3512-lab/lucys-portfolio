rep.tools

From landing page
to browsable storefront.

A brand and experience redesign for a replica-fashion toolkit, bringing its growing catalogue to the front and giving its utilities one coherent home.

The problem

The catalogue had grown to 6,900+ items, but the homepage still pitched the product. Visitors had to click through to reach what they came to browse.

The response

Make the homepage the catalogue. Put browsing first, design for visitors arriving on phones, and pair a streetwear identity with clear, readable logistics data.

My contribution

Brand identity, information architecture, responsive interface design and frontend implementation. The founder built and maintains the product.

Delivery

38 files updated. One shared stylesheet and reusable navigation and footer. Impact measurement is the next step.

01Browse immediately

Scrollable category rows, visible counts and filters put the catalogue within reach on mobile.

02Speak the culture

Condensed poster type, cyan and hard edges establish the identity; mono keeps data legible.

03Unify the toolkit

Tracking, QC photos, converters, calculators and search come together in one tabbed surface.
