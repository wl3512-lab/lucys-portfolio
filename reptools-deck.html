rep.tools

I gave a vibe-coded
site a brand of its own.

A brand identity, logo and experience redesign for a replica-fashion toolkit that worked but looked like every generated site.

The problem

The site looked vibe-coded: a gradient bolt icon, a gradient headline, glowing orbs. Bland and forgettable, for an audience that lives in streetwear culture.

The response

Give it a brand. A logo and identity from the culture (Anton caps, electric cyan, mono for data), then the homepage rebuilt as the catalogue on that identity, phone first.

My contribution

Brand identity, information architecture, responsive interface design and frontend implementation. The founder built and maintains the product.

Delivery

38 files updated. One shared stylesheet and reusable navigation and footer. Impact measurement is the next step.

01The logo uses poster type

The BATCH logo, condensed poster type, cyan and hard edges establish the identity; mono keeps data legible.

02The catalogue opens first

Scrollable category rows, visible counts and filters put the catalogue within reach on mobile.

03Every tool sits on one tabbed page

Tracking, QC photos, converters, calculators and search come together in one tabbed surface.
