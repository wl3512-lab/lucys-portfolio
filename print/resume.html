Spring – Nov 2026
  • Designed and built a browser game on when to question AI: players grow a garden with five AI animal helpers, each drifting into a real failure (sycophancy, hallucination, reckless advice, false memory, emotional manipulation) that they catch, name, and fix with a rule.
